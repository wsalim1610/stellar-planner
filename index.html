    What to read from the game

    Take your screenshots first, then copy from them. Only these change the plan noticeably:

    • Every time: the time on the screenshot, the Nova multiplier, the O count and O's levels (together they decide when the next Nova fires), and the timers of Mg, Si and Fe (and Ne if its fusion takes minutes).
    • Optional: the levels of H, He and C. Auto upgrade rebuilds them within a minute after a Nova, so they rarely change the plan.
    • When they change: Supernova and global multipliers, Ne and Mg levels, Si and Fe counts and levels.
    • Skip: the greyed fields. The counts of H, He and C are always plentiful, and Stellar Energy is spent within seconds.

      How this works
      • Every element, upgrade and multiplier follows the formulas checked against your screenshots of 8 Oct 2026.
      • H to Mg are simulated as steady flows; Si and Fe fusions run one at a time with real timers.
      • Automation buys the cheapest upgrade first, as in the game. Manual buys, bursts and unlocks happen only at your check-ins.
      • The best automatic plan tries each setting in turn (Nova and Supernova triggers, a Supernova now, purchase order, Fe automation, unlock order, auto upgrade) and keeps whatever reaches Overdrive sooner, until nothing improves.
      • The day range reruns the plan with two other Nova formulas that also fit your data: one more optimistic, one more pessimistic beyond the data.
      • Overdrive is counted at the first check-in with 64 Fe.
      • Your entries are saved in this browser only.

      Drag to get close, then fine-tune with the boxes. A few hours off on a long fusion barely changes the plan.
